How DTI impacts approval

DTI is not your credit score, but it works with your credit score. A buyer with good credit and low monthly debt can usually qualify for more home. A buyer with high car payments, credit cards, student loans, or personal loans may qualify for less even with the same income.

Income

Lenders look at stable monthly income before taxes. W2, salary, hourly income, bonus, commission, and self-employed income may be reviewed differently.

Monthly debt

Car payments, minimum credit card payments, student loans, child support, alimony, and personal loans can reduce your buying power.

Home payment

Your future mortgage payment, taxes, insurance, HOA, and mortgage insurance all count toward your housing affordability.
