Why credit matters for DPA approval

Down payment assistance is not only about needing help with the down payment. Lenders and programs still review your credit score, payment history, debt-to-income ratio, and ability to qualify for the mortgage. If your DTI works but your score is low, your next move may be fixing credit instead of waiting months.

Credit Score

Your score helps determine if your file meets minimum program and loan requirements.

Negative Items

Collections, charge-offs, late payments, and inaccurate items may weaken your approval profile.

Utilization

High credit card balances compared to limits can pull down your score even when income is strong.
