SMS Terms & Conditions

Effective October 22, 2026

The DPA Help Center PrepHub messaging program is operated by The Down Payment Assistance Help Center LLC. These terms describe the optional text messaging program.

Program and message types

With your opt-in, DPA Help Center may send recurring automated customer-care texts about your PrepHub preparation steps, account access, appointments, and requested callbacks. Message frequency varies based on your activity and requests. Message and data rates may apply. These messages do not guarantee loan approval, program eligibility, funding, or a particular assistance amount.

How you opt in

On dpahelpcenter.com, choose Learn More, answer the first-time homebuyer and location questions, and continue to the contact-information step. Enter your mobile number and select the separate, optional SMS checkbox before submitting your preparation form. The checkbox is not preselected. You can submit the form without choosing texts. Providing a number alone does not enroll you.

I agree to receive recurring automated text messages from DPA Help Center about my PrepHub preparation, account access, appointments, and requested callbacks at the number provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase or receiving services.

The form links to these SMS Terms and our Privacy Policy. Consent is specific to DPA Help Center’s PrepHub customer-care program and is not required to purchase or receive services. Do not opt in using a number you do not own or have authority to use.

Stop messages

Reply STOP to the number sending you messages at any time to opt out. You may receive one final message confirming your request; then no further messages from that program will be sent unless you opt in again. Contact us if you need help changing your number or preferences.

Help and support

Reply HELP for help, email info@dpahelpcenter.com, or call 1-833-302-8953.

Delivery and privacy

Delivery depends on your mobile carrier and network availability. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. See the Privacy Policy for how we handle your information. Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Please do not send sensitive financial documents or account details by text.

Changes

We may revise these terms by publishing an updated version here. If the messaging purpose changes materially, we will obtain any new consent required before sending those messages.
