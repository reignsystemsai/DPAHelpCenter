Why taxes matter for DPA approval

Down payment assistance still requires mortgage approval. Lenders need to verify the income used to qualify you, especially if you are self-employed, paid by 1099, own a business, or have income that changes from year to year.

Two tax paths for homebuyers

Your tax documentation depends on how you are paid. W2 employees usually have a simpler review, while self-employed and 1099 borrowers may need more documentation.

Path 1

W2 Employee

If you are paid through an employer, lenders typically review paystubs, W2s, and employment history.
