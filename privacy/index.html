Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Down Payment Assistance Help Center LLC (“DPA Help Center,” “we,” or “us”) uses information provided through dpahelpcenter.com, PrepHub, and our related communications.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, purchase location and home-price goal, requested assistance amount, and self-reported credit, income, employment, and tax-filing information. If you use PrepHub, we also collect saved workbook entries, documents you choose to upload, and preparation progress. Communications may include messages, call outcomes, notes, transcripts, and recordings when enabled and handled under applicable requirements. Our systems also process technical information needed to operate the site, such as device/browser information, access logs, session information, and cookies or local storage.

How we use information

We use information to respond to your inquiry, provide account access, prepare and maintain your PrepHub plan, support your preparation steps, arrange requested follow-ups, operate our CRM and automated assistance, improve services, and protect against misuse. Readiness scores and estimates support preparation; they are not loan approvals or guarantees of assistance.

Service providers and referrals

We use service providers to host the website and CRM, store records, deliver email and calls or texts, and provide AI-assisted services. They receive information needed to perform those functions on our behalf. If you ask us to connect you with a lender, real estate professional, tax professional, or another specialist, we may share relevant information to facilitate that requested referral. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect rights and security.

Text messaging privacy

We use your mobile number to send text messages only for the messaging program you opt into. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell SMS opt-in information or consent. Service providers may process mobile information solely to deliver and support our messaging service.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won’t be shared with any third parties.

SMS consent is optional and separate from requesting assistance. We retain records of the consent wording and version, your selection, the number provided, the source page, and submission time. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. See our SMS Terms & Conditions.

Your choices

You can decline texts and still request assistance or use our services. To stop texts, reply STOP to the sending number. To update contact preferences, request access or correction, or ask about deletion of your information, contact info@dpahelpcenter.com. We may verify your identity before responding. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security, or operational purposes.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as needed to provide services, maintain communication and consent records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations. We use access controls and other safeguards appropriate to our systems, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Do not send Social Security numbers, full financial account numbers, tax documents, or credit reports by SMS.

Children and policy updates

Our services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy by publishing a revised version and effective date here.
